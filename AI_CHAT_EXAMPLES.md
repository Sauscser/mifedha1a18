## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Be Specific:** "What is Pal-Pal?" works better than "Tell me about products"
2. **Ask Naturally:** Use conversational language like "How can this help me?"
3. **Use Keywords:** Include product names or features (loans, savings, shopping, etc.)
4. **Ask Follow-ups:** Bot remembers context and can answer follow-up questions
5. **Request Navigation:** Say "Take me to [product]" to jump to that screen
